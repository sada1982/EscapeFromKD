3F lighting and stage component read-only audit
2026-11-13, /Game/Main/Stage/KDStage
Normal unreal-mcp tools only. No Editor changes, UI, camera, or PIE actions.

Facts:
- 42 RectLights and 4 PointLights; all Movable.
- All 42 RectLights cast shadows, static-object shadows, and dynamic-object shadows.
- Every light has maxDrawDistance=0 and maxDistanceFadeRange=0 (no configured light distance culling).
- Every light affects translucency, GI, and reflections.
- Every light uses project-controlled ray-traced shadow choice; none forces distance-field shadows.
- MegaLights is allowed on every light, but that alone does not mean MegaLights is enabled globally.
- Main classroom: 12 shadowed RectLights, radius450cm, 1900lm.
- Closed adjacent room: 9 shadowed RectLights, radius450cm, 1900lm.
- Closed CAD room: 9 shadowed RectLights, radius450cm, 1900lm.
- Gallery: 9 shadowed RectLights, radius330cm, 1100lm.
- Atrium: 1 shadowed RectLight, radius1400cm, 14000lm.
- Stair: 1 shadowed RectLight, radius650cm, 1100lm.
- Lift: 1 shadowed RectLight, radius450cm, 1100lm.
- Three atrium fill PointLights: radius1800cm, 18000/12000/8000lm, shadows disabled.
- Stair fill PointLight: radius650cm, 2000lm, shadows disabled.
- Main classroom center lies inside all12 classroom light spheres, the atrium RectLight sphere, and all3 large atrium fill spheres: upper-bound overlap16.
- Radius overlap is not a measured contributing-light count. Walls, shape, frustum and occlusion still matter.
- One unbound PostProcessVolume: only AutoExposureMinBrightness=6, MaxBrightness=6 and Bias=0 are explicitly overridden.
- PP stored Lumen/MegaLights values are not active overrides.
- Stage actor has12 StaticMeshComponents. All are Static, bCanEverTick=false, boundsScale=1, no parent-bound override.
- All12 components cast shadows and participate in ray tracing/distance-field lighting. All have distance-cull limit0.
- Stage transforms are identity; no per-component material overrides.

Priority proposals for root to A/B measure:
1. Profile shadow and local-light GPU passes before mesh simplification. The 42 shadowed movable RectLights are the strongest scene-specific candidate.
2. Compare existing VSM/local-light setup with MegaLights if supported on this project/GPU. Existing lights already permit it. Confirm effective global CVar before interpreting PP defaults.
3. Establish a sparse set of shadow-casting key lights, retaining other lights as fill only where light leakage and self-shadow change are acceptable. Keep stair/door safety lighting. This must be visually checked; disabling every shadow blindly would materially alter the scene.
4. Add finite light draw distance/fade to inaccessible-room and distant-local lights, preserving them while their room is visible. Start from room dimensions and measured viewpoints, not an arbitrary global aggressive limit.
5. Review the three1800cm atrium fill lights: their spheres extend to the main classroom and lobby. Reducing influence or overlap could reduce shading cost, but compare exposures because they currently brighten the surrounding floor.
6. Evaluate whether local lights all need translucency lighting. Do not disable globally without checking the glazed classroom/atrium boundaries.
7. Stage components are already static/non-ticking and only12 in number. Converting them to Static or disabling Tick yields no gain. Model-splitting/LOD/material work is owned by the model audit.
8. Do not simply convert lights to Static: DefaultEngine.ini currently disables static lighting; bake workflow would need explicit additional project changes, lightmap validation, and rebuild.
